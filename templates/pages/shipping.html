Shipping & Returns

Shipping Rates

We offer complimentary standard shipping on all orders within Kenya. International shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on your destination country.

Delivery Times

  • Nairobi: 1–2 business days.
  • Other Kenyan Counties: 2–4 business days.
  • International: 5–10 business days.

Returns Policy

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and have all original tags attached.
